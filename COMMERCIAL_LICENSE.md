## When you need a commercial licence

The AGPL lets anyone run, study, modify and share Entroptics RF. In return, a modified version you
distribute, or let users interact with over a network, must be offered with its complete source
under the AGPL. A commercial licence removes that obligation.

You need a commercial licence if you:

- build Entroptics RF, or a modified version of it, into a radio, firmware image or product whose
  source you do not release under the AGPL;
- keep modifications closed-source while distributing them, or while offering them to users over a
  network;
- need patent rights for use outside the AGPL's terms (see [PATENTS.md](PATENTS.md));
- want support, warranty, indemnity or certification work.

Running Entroptics RF unmodified, and modifying it for your own internal use without distributing it or
offering it over a network, needs no agreement.

("built on Entroptics", "Powered by Entroptics") does not.
